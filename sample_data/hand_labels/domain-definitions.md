# Domain definitions

Written by Edvard Tokle on 2026-11-05, before he labelled the 50 Markets in `markets.csv` blind, in the same sitting (PRD FR-19, spine AD-16). They are never edited. The language model's prompt quotes the five lines below word for word, and the tag rules of Mock LLM Mode are written from them.

One spelling fix with his ok: "n" is written as "and". Nothing else is changed.

- **sports**: physical competitive real life games
- **politics**: country and organisation stuff, geopolitics counts as politics
- **crypto**: on chain coins
- **macro**: fed policy, rate cuts, inflation
- **other**: everything else, pop culture, tech, single company stocks
