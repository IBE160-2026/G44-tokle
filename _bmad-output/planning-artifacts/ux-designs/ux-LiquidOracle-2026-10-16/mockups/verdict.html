About this chart

Each replay (a Simulation) keeps this Wallet's 212 resolved bets, prices and stakes, but lets every bet win or lose by chance, at the odds its price implied. That is one way a trader with no edge could have done.

The bars count how many of the 10,000 replays ended at each result. On average, luck alone lands at $0.
