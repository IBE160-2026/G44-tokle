## 4. Review

Three reviewer helpers filed 26 findings. The AI checked each against the code. No question needed Edvard; 11 were fixed in 10 changes, 1 was deferred, and the rest were rejected or repeats. The most useful:
- **A real bug:** if the app was started before `load-sample` (the app's own "not loaded" page tells you to run it then), an offline Scan only saw the 8 committed Wallets. The offline data source now looks for new files first. Found by two reviewers.
- **Memory:** building the holder lists kept about 1,000 coin-flipper Wallets in memory at once. They are now read one at a time.
- **Missing tests:** nothing checked that `run --offline` really serves the offline Scans page and marks a Scan cut off by closing the app as interrupted. And the accessibility checks for "the offline Scans page" were quietly looking at the live form. Both are now tested.
- Smaller fixes: an unused parameter deleted, a known limit written down for Story 4.7 (the holder lists only count plain trades), a typo, clearer names, and one step added to the examiner's offline flow in EXPERIENCE.md.

**Deferred:** an old open question in EXPERIENCE.md (the default Cut-off Date) still reads as unsettled, though it was settled in October. It was there before this story.

**Rejected, for example:** making the Scan list count only the 1,004 findable Wallets (the approved spec says the whole Sample Dataset; it closes with 4.7), and guarding against someone hand-editing the volume list. All are in the spec's triage log.

**Checks that the new tests work:** the AI planted bugs on purpose, one at a time, and ran the tests each time: five while building, and five for the review fixes.
