## 2. Planning

- **Helpers.** The AI asked whether to use helpers as in 4.1 (code search, build, review), and recommended it. **Edvard chose the same as 4.1.**

Its Gamma query and row limit became the plan. Its saved files held only trimmed rows, so tests use the fake Polymarket instead of real answers.

The AI asked two questions, each with a recommendation:

1. **How many rows may a Market's holder list have before the Market is skipped** (`MARKET_ROW_CAP`)? A partly read list leans towards winners or losers, so an over-limit Market is "not used: partial".

   - (c) 5,000.

   **Edvard chose (a)** (AI rec).
2. **How is a Wallet that Polymarket refuses (not a contract) ranked?** The plan said only "kept and flagged".
   - (a) On what was read ($0 when nothing was), the same rule as every Wallet;
   - (b) always in the top N.

   **Edvard chose (a)** (AI rec).

**Spec size.** The spec was about 2,300 tokens against the 1,600 guideline. The AI recommended keeping it whole; **Edvard kept it whole** ("1a 2a keep"), then said "approve and continue".

## 3. Build

A fresh helper built from the spec. Choices it made beyond the spec are listed in the spec's Implementation Notes. One example: a Market list Polymarket refuses makes that Market "not used" instead of stopping the Scan.

Checks:
- **Leak test.** It flips the result of every Market resolved after the date, and the funnel's result must not change. A planted twin shows it would catch a ranking on the whole history.
- **Mutations.** The helper broke the code five ways on purpose, and tests caught each one.
- **Live check.** The online test (`tests/online`, never run on GitHub) matched the spike's 100 Markets exactly.

## 4. Review

Three reviewer helpers filed 24 findings: 3 fixed, 1 deferred, 20 rejected with a reason. The AI checked each against the code first.

**Fixed:**
- **A real bug in AI-written code.** The Market list was cut to 100 before it was sorted by volume, so a bigger Market arriving late on the last page could be dropped. The fix sorts first, with a new test that fails on the old order.
- **A weak test.** The test for a Wallet that was not fully read only checked that its profit differed from the full value, so ranking it on $0 would still have passed. It now checks the exact number.
- **A docstring that promised too much.** A refusal at any point discards everything read for that Wallet, so it always ranks at $0.

**Deferred:** a holder row naming an outcome its Market does not have would stop the whole Scan with an error. Nobody knows whether Polymarket ever sends one (the spike kept no token ids), so it is in `deferred-work.md` for Story 4.3.

Every finding is in the spec's triage log.

## 5. What the AI got wrong, and how it was caught

- **Cut before sort** in the Market list: found by two reviewers, fixed with a test.
- **The weak not-fully-read test**: found by the verification reviewer.
- **A time estimate that was easy to misread.** When Edvard chose the row limit, the AI said the worst case was "about 10 minutes of reading". That is only the Market lists. A Scan also reads up to 3 × N Wallets, which a reviewer pointed out takes much longer. The AI corrected this to Edvard at the end of the session.
