## 6. What the helpers found in the PRD (for Edvard to decide later)

Both helpers agreed with the app everywhere, but they named three places where the PRD does not quite say what the app does. They are in `deferred-work.md`:
1. **Fees at an old rate.** Every fee that fails the fee check fits Polymarket's fee formula at 1.67× to 7× the rate listed now. That fits the rate change found in Story 2.6. The strict check then throws away fees that were real when paid (0.28 for 0x0f4b0a, 44.48 for 0x091596).
2. **Fees on shares later sold** count in full in Real PnL (636.90 of 0x091596's 1,087.34). That is what the Glossary literally says, but FR-3 puts sold shares in Exit PnL.
3. **"Converted shares in that Market"**: a conversion row names the whole event, and the app leaves out the whole event (Edvard's Story 2.4 call). The helper reached the same reading from the data alone, but the PRD wording could say so.
