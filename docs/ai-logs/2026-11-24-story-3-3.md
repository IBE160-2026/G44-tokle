## 2. Planning

The first commit set Story 3.2 to done in the sprint status (834e36b), as in earlier stories.

`bmad-build` loaded the Epic 3 context and the 3.2 spec, then looked at the code. Its main finding: the numbers the notes need (bets left out per reason, failed fees, window size) were already computed by the one Position builder, but the lookup threw that record away and kept only the Skill Test result. So the plan was to keep that record and turn it into finished sentences in a new `services/honesty.py`, the same way `services/chart.py` does for the chart.

It also found a problem in the plan itself: the window line was planned as "Based on the latest 2,000 of N bets", but the app stops reading a Wallet's history once it has 2,000 bets (on purpose, for speed), so it never knows N. The database did not even record whether the cap applied.

Before writing the spec, the AI ran the planned rules on every stored Sample Wallet, so the questions could use real numbers.

The spec had four open questions, each with a recommendation:
1. **The window line's "of N".** (a) drop N and store a "capped" flag (the database is rebuilt, so `load-sample` runs once more); (b) read the whole history to count N (slow, and impossible offline); (c) "of at least N". **Edvard chose (a)**, the AI's recommendation.
2. **Where to explain the +1** (his own note from 3.2). (a) one sentence at the end of the About paragraph that only shows with JavaScript, which is also the only time the tail label shows; (b) in the honesty notes; (c) leave it. **Edvard chose (a)**, the AI's recommendation.
3. **What "N of M positions held to resolution" counts.** (a) the tested bets, as in the mockup; (b) every bet not sold before resolution. The AI recommended (b), because with (a) the 287 split-or-merged bets of the real Wallet 0xd3a6e5 would read as not held. **Edvard chose (b), but added two checks: that the AI's example numbers actually add up, and that bets still open never count as held.** The AI checked both: 641 tested + 139 Slivers + 287 split or merged + 95 sold + 6 lists disagree + 5 unclear = 1,173, and 939 held. Every stored Wallet has 0 bets in an open Market, because the window only takes closed Markets. Polymarket's "OPEN" list just means not cashed in. While checking, the AI found one grey area and asked a follow-up: bets the app could not read in full, where it can't see whether they were sold. **Edvard chose to leave them out of both N and M** (the AI's recommendation), rather than counting them as held or as not held.
4. **What the Exit PnL total includes** (open since Story 1.2). (a) only bets whose fills the app trusts: tested bets, Slivers and bets sold before resolution, no sell fees; (b) every bet with a sale, split-or-merged included. On the real Wallet 0xcf0d7f this is −$195,091 against −$23,285. **Edvard chose (a)**, the AI's recommendation.

The spec was about 2,200 tokens, over the 1,600 guideline. The AI recommended keeping it whole, since the warning and the card share the same data plumbing. **Edvard: keep it whole, "approve and continue".**

## 4. Review

Three reviewer helpers read the diff and filed 31 findings. 8 groups were fixed, 1 was deferred, and the rest were rejected with a reason (all in the spec's triage log). The main fixes:
- **A missing window line.** If the app stopped reading with exactly 2,000 bets collected, it said "not capped", although older bets were never read. A new test with a small fake Polymarket failed on the old rule and passes now.
- **"Latest 2,003 bets".** The window line counted tokens found only in the trade history, so it could say more than the 2,000 cap. It now counts what the cap counts.
- **Claims the app can't back.** For a Wallet where nothing was read in full (0xd218e4), the card still said "No shares were sold before resolution." and "Every fee matched the Market's fee schedule." Both are now left out there.
- **Rounding.** 28.5% showed as "28%" because of computer float maths; it now shows "29%".
- **Edge cases of the largest-bet sentence.** A Real PnL shown as "$0", and a bet beating the Real PnL by a fraction of a cent, now read sensibly.
- **Plan wording.** The PRD bullet now says what M counts without contradicting itself, and EXPERIENCE.md lists every reason name the card uses.
- **More page-level tests:** the losing Wallet's amount column, a capped real Wallet's page, the linked-bets note, and 0xcf0d7f's −$195,091 from the decision log.

**Rejected, among others:** one reviewer argued that bets with unclear resolution or other problems should not count as "held". That is exactly what Edvard decided at spec time, so it stays. **Deferred:** the "first 2,000" wording for after parts and Watchlist results, which can't exist before Epics 4 and 5.
