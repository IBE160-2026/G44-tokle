## 4. Review

Three reviewer helpers filed 31 findings. The AI checked each against the code and, for the ones about leaks, against the real capture too. No question needed Edvard; 19 were fixed in 12 changes, 1 was deferred, and the rest were rejected. The most useful were all about Edvard's no-peeking check:
- **A late "conversion" could slip through.** On Polymarket, a conversion swaps shares across a whole group of linked Markets, so its row carries the group's id, not the Market's. The check compared Market ids only. It now matches the group's id too.
- **"Combo" trade rows were invisible to it**, because the app drops them when reading trades. The build now keeps their Market and time for the check.
- **It trusted the capture to be complete.** It now stops if the capture did not read the Wallet's trades for the whole time after the cut-off.
- Smaller fixes: the funnel list's own resolution time is checked too, a test on the committed files no longer claims more than it can see, the Scan count is now pinned by a test, `dataset.json` records where the real volume list came from, and two docstrings were corrected.

On today's data none of these holes hid anything: 47 of the 50 Markets are in such linked groups, and none has a late conversion; there are no late combo rows; every capture covers the time after the cut-off. The stricter build still passes.

**One sentence in the approved spec overstated a point.** The spec said a later payout leaves "the pre-filter's value the same as at the cut-off". That is true for a bet held to the end, but not exactly when part of a bet was sold before the date. No information from after the date gets in, and a live Scan today reads the same rows. The spec text is locked after approval, so the AI corrected only the code's docstring and reported it. **Edvard then had the spec sentence fixed too** ("go ahead n fix that sentence in the spec, im ok with it, just note the change in the change log"); the change is in the spec's change log.

**Deferred:** the 1,018 against 1,012 count on the Scans page (above), which comes from 4.6.

**Rejected, for example:** guarding against a malformed capture file (it is checked against its SHA-256 first), reading the volume lists less often (small files), and Markets with three or more outcomes (all funnel Markets are yes/no). All are in the spec's triage log.

**Checks that the new tests work:** the AI planted bugs on purpose, one at a time, and ran the tests each time: five while building (no late-row check, late counted from the wrong time, real rows worked out from trades too, before/after parts not walked, token taken from the wrong price) and five for the review fixes (conversion by Market id only, combo rows not checked, no completeness check, the Gamma side of the resolution check dropped, no list-name guard). All ten were caught.
