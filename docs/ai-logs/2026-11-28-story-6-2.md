## 2. Planning

The AI read the plan for Story 6.2 and the code it touches, and drafted the spec. Two things in the plan were open or said two ways, and it asked instead of choosing:

1. **A bet bought in several fills, where one fill has no price at a delay.** The PRD says the Position is left out; a line written for Story 6.1 said "a buy". Options: (a) leave the whole bet out of that delay and count it, or (b) leave out only the fills without a price. The AI recommended (a): a half-priced bet would set a full outcome against part of its cost. **Edvard chose (a).**
2. **Which fee rate the follower pays**, left open since Story 2.8. Options: (a) the rate Polymarket lists for the Market today, with the README saying so, or (b) the rate the copied Wallet's own fill fitted. The AI recommended (a): (b) is only known where the Wallet paid a taker fee. **Edvard chose (a).**
3. **Helpers** (separate AI sessions that know nothing of this chat): one for the reference calculation, which the plan requires, and helpers for the review; the AI builds the rest. **Edvard: yes.**
4. **Spec size**, about 3,500 tokens against BMAD's guide of 1,600. The AI recommended keeping it whole, because a split costs one more pull request and both halves are needed before Edvard's stop rule (Story 6.3 merged by midday on 30 November). **Edvard kept it whole.**

His answer: "1a 2a 3 yes 4a, approve and continue".

Choices the AI made itself and listed for him before he approved:
- **Who is priced:** every Wallet that passed before the Cut-off Date, and every other Wallet of the Scan with at least 10 bets after it. The rest can never be in the group the luck check draws from, so their prices would be fetched for nothing. On the live Scan that is 118 of 200 Wallets.
- **A new Scan that stops while fetching prices is not finished** and resumes as today. A Scan that was already finished stays finished and only gains Copy results.
- **The time estimate on the Scans page** gains about 50 price requests per Wallet, from the 9,644 bets measured on the live Scan.
- **A real-data check that reads counts only:** one real Wallet is copied live on a duplicate of the kept database, and only how many bets were priced is read. Its money numbers are not looked at before Story 6.6, so the real result is not seen before the luck check exists.

The decisions went into `epics.md`, the Glossary, the architecture (AD-5, AD-6, AD-15, AD-22), EXPERIENCE.md and three memlogs, committed (d09e895) before any code.

## 6. Review

Three helpers read the diff without knowing how it was built: 26 findings (15, 5 and 6). None found a wrong follower number. The ones that mattered:

- **The time estimate and the new job** (all three found it): every job of a Scan counted in its time. A Copy Test job that stopped would have made the next estimate too long, and one the app was closed on would have thrown the Scan's own known time away. Fixed, with tests.
- **Two Wallets holding the same bet were never tested together**, although that is the normal case on real data. A helper showed a one-line bug that every test passed. A test now plants two Wallets that bought the same 100 outcomes six hours apart; the helper's bug is caught.
- **The plan still said the rule Edvard had rejected** in one place (FR-18c: "a buy ... is left out"), the Glossary's fee formula was missing its exponent, and how a sale is spread over a bet's fills was written nowhere. All fixed in the plan.
- **Buys just before a Market closes** had no rule and no test. The plan as written gives one (use the recorded price if there is one, else leave the bet out); it is now written down and tested, and named as a question for a later story.
- **Tests that proved less than they said**, three of them, including one whose description said "asks for prices only" while nothing was asked at all. Fixed.
- **Rejected (3):** a complaint that the spec was not in the reviewed diff (the workflow keeps it from two of the three helpers on purpose), and two true remarks whose only fix is editing the approved spec's task lines; they are in the spec's notes instead.

Six more planted bugs for these fixes: all caught.

## 7. After the review

The AI put one finding to Edvard without building it, because fixing it changes a rule he had approved:

- **If a bug (not a Polymarket outage) hits the Copy phase of a new Scan**, the Scan failed like in any other phase, and a failed job has no Resume. A long Scan would be lost at its last step, with its Watchlist and Hold-up results already worked out. Options: (a) keep it as approved, or (b) log the error and finish the Scan without a Copy Test, which the Copy Test job can add later. The AI recommended (b), because the Copy phase is the one that depends on price data the app has seen least of.

It also asked him to confirm two readings it had written into the plan during the review:

1. A bet partly sold before it resolved: the shares still held are split over its buys in proportion to their size.
2. A buy just before a Market closes: no special rule; a recorded price is used, and without one the bet is left out and counted.

**Edvard answered: "b, 1 yes 2 yes, commit n push".**

The AI built (b): the approved spec got one new rule and one new table row by his decision, with an entry in its change log, and the architecture (AD-5) and `epics.md` say it. An outage still stops the Scan with Resume. A test plants a bug in the phase of a new Scan, checks that the Scan finishes with its other results, and then gives it its Copy Test from the job. Three more planted bugs against this change were all caught.
