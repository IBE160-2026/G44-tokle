## 5. Step 4: final validation

The AI checked its own 38 stories rule by rule and found three real problems:
- the negative-risk coupling was in two stories;
- Edvard's 30-bet guard was in a story that came before the page it had to be tested through;
- the Sample Dataset story needed Cut-off windows from a later epic.

It fixed all three without changing any decision, and closed smaller gaps: Market records that never expire, the database migration, "a new Cut-off Date is a new Scan", the Seed on the Copy Test page, the key never logged, tests that name their requirement, and the stage closing checks.

It also found that **Edvard's Epic 1 slip rule conflicted with the dependencies**: the number-phrases story could slip, but the Verdict card needs it. Edvard decided: "1.5 stays before 1.7, only 1.7s polish slips".

The AI then pointed to Edvard's own lesson from the architecture step ("never let the author be the only check") and recommended an independent review before completing. **Edvard said yes, but in the next session**, so the workflow's final [C] is not pressed yet.
