## 3. What the checks found

- **A wrong win on real data.** The AI grouped 0x091596's bets by Gamma event and found that its conversion in the Romania election event was being thrown away: the CONVERSION row carries the event's id, not a Market's id. So the converted NO bet (1,666.67 shares at $0.36) was tested as a held winner, about +$1,066 that never existed. Now all 25 bets of that event are "split or merged". Its Real PnL goes from −$48,618.97 to −$49,571.39, and tested bets from 17 to 14. Story 2.7's hand calculation for this Wallet must follow.
- **Negative-risk events are common.** 0x091596 has 11 of them in its window, 0x29306d 4. Most bets in them are cheap long shots or NO bets near 0.99, so the stretches sum to between 0.02 and 0.75: the "side by side" layout fits real data.
- **The "not linked" share** on the fixtures: 0x29306d 0 of 18, 0x091596 2 of 14 (a match's over/under and its winner, same event), 0x0f4b0a 3 of 11 (three Markets about one TV season). At 27%, 0x0f4b0a is the one fixture over the 20% note.
- **`load-sample`** still loads all 1,005 Sample Wallets (18-20 s). The database version went up, so an old `offline.db` is rebuilt and needs `load-sample` once more.

## 4. Bugs found in the AI's code

Before the review, the AI broke each new rule on purpose, 21 changes in all, and checked that a test failed. **Two did not**: using the sum of a Market's two sides instead of the larger one (the AI's own test had space to spare), and the window keeping any row that names an event, not just conversions. A test was added for each. One long Python edit script in a bash heredoc also broke on quoting, and the edits were redone with the editor tool.

The three reviewers raised 20 findings. The AI checked each against the code:

| Found by | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Verification-gap and blind reviewers | No fixed ("golden") count pinned the new shared draw, so a change in the layout's order would pass every test; the tests also ran a copy of the engine's setup | A golden count through the real engine; the engine and the test helper share one ordering and one random-number setup |
| Blind and edge-case reviewers | A negative-risk Market with odd data was left uncoupled silently | It is now logged as a warning |
| Edge-case reviewer | Yes/No outcomes with three token ids would have been coupled | Coupled only with exactly two tokens |
| Blind reviewer | The builder let a SPLIT or MERGE mark a whole event, not only a CONVERSION | Only a CONVERSION can, with a test |
| Blind reviewer | Two helper functions defined "coupled" slightly differently | One definition used everywhere |
| Blind reviewer | The architecture still stated the ladder rule as current; two comments were incomplete | Reworded |

All three reviewers also asked what happens if a negative-risk Market is ever not Yes/No: its conversion would be missed. The AI checked the raw data: 108 of 108 such Markets are Yes/No. It is deferred to Story 2.6, which checks it on the 10 real Wallets. Six findings were rejected with reasons; for example "Slivers should count in the share" was rejected because the plan defines the share over tested bets, and a Sliver holds under one share. After the fixes the AI reran the deliberate-break check with 25 changes, and every one failed a test. The full table is in the spec's Review Triage Log.
