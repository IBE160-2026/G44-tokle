## 2. Planning

The AI read the code and the plan itself, with no helper. It found three things the plan did not settle, and asked four questions, each with a recommendation:

1. **What "reuse stored Wallet data" means for a new Cut-off Date.** The PRD said "only Wallets not seen before are fetched", but a Wallet's bets before one date are a different set from its bets before another date.
   - (a) Reuse only for the same Wallet, date and side, while under 24 hours old; never fetch a Market record twice; reword the PRD.
   - (b) Build a new date's data from data stored for another date.

   **Edvard chose (a)** (AI rec: (b) is new, risky logic five days before the code freeze).
2. **How big one "unit of work" is.** A waiting lookup goes first between two units. The plan said one Market-list page; 4.2 had built a whole Market.
   - (a) Whole Market, and reword the architecture.
   - (b) Page by page, which changes 4.2's merged code.

   **Edvard chose (a)** (AI rec). The AI said a lookup would then wait "at most about 6 seconds". The review later showed that this was only true for Markets (see section 5).
3. **Spec size.** About 2,400 tokens against the 1,600 guideline. **Edvard kept it whole** (AI rec) rather than splitting it into parts A and B.
4. **Helpers.** **Edvard chose helpers for the review only** (AI rec), as in 4.1 and 4.2.

## 4. Review

Three reviewer helpers filed 28 findings, 22 after merging duplicates. The AI checked each against the code: 11 fixed, none deferred, the rest rejected with a reason. All are in the spec's triage log.

**Fixed:**
- **A real gap in AI-written code: the "as-of" time was saved but never used.** The architecture (AD-19) says a Scan ignores Markets resolved after it started. A Scan resumed days later would have counted them, so its results could differ from an uninterrupted run. Now those Markets are left out, with two tests.
- **IDs could be reused after a schema change.** If a future version could not copy a Scan's data, new snapshots would restart at id 1 and could take a rebuilt Scan's old ids, showing another Wallet's data. The id counters are now carried over.
- **Progress never reached the total** for the Market and Wallet steps ("3 of 4" was the last message). It now ends at "4 of 4".
- **Six missing tests** found by the verification reviewer: a lookup going first between Wallets and between Skill Tests; Resume during the Skill Test step; a second Scan reusing data; a refused after part; "1,200 of 18,000" formatting; and counting requests per Wallet on Resume. The AI planted 8 more bugs to check the new tests, and all were caught.

**Rejected, for example:** checks on the Cut-off Date and the Number of Wallets (Story 4.4's form does them before anything starts), and Offline Mode Scans (Story 4.6). A full list with reasons is in the spec.

**Also closed:** 4.2's deferred item, a holder row naming an outcome its Market doesn't have. That now skips the Market instead of stopping the Scan.

## 5. What the AI got wrong, and how it was caught

- **The as-of time stored but unused**: found by the blind reviewer, fixed with tests.
- **A test gap the AI's own checks found**: `scan_whole` ignoring the after part passed every test until the AI planted that bug and added the split-Wallet test.
- **"A lookup waits at most about 6 seconds."** The AI told Edvard this when he chose whole Markets. It is only true for a Market. A Wallet is one unit too, and a big Wallet's read takes much longer. Edvard's choice stands, since Wallets were always whole units in the plan. The architecture and the Epic 4 summary now say this. The spec's frozen section still had the old line; the AI asked, and **Edvard approved correcting it** ("yes fix the spec line").
- **Small slips the tests caught during the build**: a test that didn't name its requirement (caught by the project's own architecture test), and a test that wrote into a pretend future schema the wrong way.
