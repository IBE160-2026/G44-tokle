## 3. The build

- `core/phrases.py`: a bar's range ("$30,000 to $35,000"), "289 replays", and the tail sentence with its two edge cases ("No replay did this well or better", "All 10,000 replays did this well or better").
- `services/chart.py`: for each bar, its range and count, plus which bar holds the Wallet's result, which bar the highlight starts at (from the exact stored count), and which side of the line the tail label goes.
- `verdict.html`: the slider, the button, the tail label, the tooltip, the new hint and the About paragraph, all hidden until the script shows them.
- `chart.js` (new): adapted from the mockup's prototype.
- `progress.js`: after a live lookup it now hands the new chart to `chart.js`, because scripts in swapped-in HTML don't run.
- `README.md`: a line on `chart.js`.

**Checked in a real browser.** With the app running in Offline Mode, a throwaway Playwright script drove Edge through 45 checks: no JavaScript, reduced motion, the build-up, Tab order, every slider key, Esc, pin and unpin, the announcements, three Wallets, and 1200, 600 and 360 px widths. **Problems found in the browser and fixed:**

## 4. Review

Three reviewer helpers read the diff and filed 26 findings; 9 groups were fixed, 1 deferred, the rest rejected with a reason (all in the spec's triage log). The main fixes:
- **Tooltip off the card.** Next to the blue line, a tooltip at the end bars could run off the card. It now tries three places and takes the first one that stays on the card and clear of the line's dot and label. This was checked for every bar of three Wallets at three widths.
- **Esc lost the slider's place.** After Esc, the next arrow key jumped back to the Wallet's bar. The slider now remembers where it was.
- **Touch screens.** A tap on a bar showed the Wallet's bar instead of the tapped one. While checking that fix, the AI found that touch also fired a "pointer left" signal on every tap, which hid the tooltip after 300 ms. Both are fixed: a tap shows the tapped bar, and the tooltip stays.
- **Printing.** A page printed before the chart scrolled into view would show no bars. It now prints in full.
- Smaller fixes:
  - Alt/Ctrl + arrow keys are left to the browser (Alt+Left is Back).
  - The README says how to pin the highlight (Tab, Enter, click, Esc).
  - The tests check more: the tail label's side, and that `chart.js` has no hidden strings.

Rejected with reasons, among them: lighting the Wallet's whole bar (the UX plan asks for exactly that) and the double "Highlight on" announcement (also asked for by the plan). Deferred: an automated test that runs `chart.js` itself. It joins the existing item from Story 2.5 for Story 3.4, as Edvard chose in 3a.

## 5. Edvard's own browser check

Edvard tried the chart himself. On sharp trader, the blue line said "1 of 10,000 replays did this well or better" while the Verdict said "about 1 time in 5,000", and he asked which one is right. The AI explained that both are: the tail label is the raw count, and the odds use the PRD's p-value, (1 + count) / (1 + 10,000) = 2 / 10,001. The +1 counts the Wallet's own result as one more try, so a Wallet that beats every replay never gets odds of zero. The AI also agreed it could confuse a reader and gave three options: (a) leave it (its recommendation, since the plan asks for both sentences as they are), (b) add a line to "About this chart" now, as a small plan change, or (c) note it for Story 3.3 (the honesty notes). **Edvard chose (c)**, not the AI's recommendation. It is in `deferred-work.md`.

The AI also recommended keeping the throwaway browser-check script out of the repo for now and deciding it with the Story 3.4 test-tool question; Edvard approved the commit plan without it ("ok commit and push").
